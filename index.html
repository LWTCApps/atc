Listen to the airspace you'll work in.

Live air traffic control from six Florida airports, for Avionics students and staff. Hear how clearances, handoffs and readbacks sound on real frequencies.

  • GroundDirects taxiing aircraft and vehicles.

Audio source

All ATC audio on this site is supplied by LiveATC.net, a network of volunteer receivers. LWTC does not record or rebroadcast it.

Pick an airport, then press play

Controllers only speak when there is traffic, so silence is normal, especially on Ground and late at night. At smaller airports, one controller often handles several positions, so some feeds are combined. For listening and training only, never for navigation or flight planning.
